Breathtaking Destinations

From the iconic savannas to the pristine coastal shores, travel to Kenya's most sought-after locations in unrivaled luxury.

Wildlife & Safari
Narok County, Kenya
From KSh 12,000/person

Maasai Mara Safari

3–7 Days Guided Safari

Witness the Greatest Show on Earth — the wildebeest migration across the Mara River. Track the Big Five, sleep under canvas in luxury tented camps, and experience Kenya's most iconic wildlife destination.

Wildlife & Photography
Kajiado County, Kenya
From KSh 10,000/person

Amboseli Explorer

2–5 Days Guided Safari

Kenya's most photogenic park. Elephant herds in the foreground, the snow-capped peak of Kilimanjaro behind — Amboseli delivers breathtaking scenery and intimate wildlife encounters unlike anywhere else.

Beach & Coastal Paradise
South Coast, Mombasa
From KSh 8,500/person

Diani Beach Escape

3–10 Days Resort & Leisure

Powder-white sands, warm turquoise Indian Ocean waters, and swaying palms line Kenya's most beautiful beach. Perfect for couples, families, and anyone seeking sun-soaked coastal paradise relaxation.

Adventure & Trekking
Central Kenya
From KSh 9,000/person

Mount Kenya Trek

4–7 Days Guided Trek

Scale Africa's second-highest peak through dramatic moorlands, glacial valleys, and alpine meadows. Whether you summit Point Lenana or explore the forest foothills, Mount Kenya is an adventure of a lifetime.

Birds & Wildlife
Nakuru County, Kenya
From KSh 7,500/person

Lake Nakuru & Flamingos

1–3 Days Day Trip Available

A flamingo-fringed alkaline lake that turns pink at the horizon. Spot rhinos, lions, and baboons in one of Kenya's most diverse parks, just two hours from Nairobi. The perfect weekend escape.

Heritage & Island Living
Lamu Archipelago, Kenya
From KSh 15,000/person

Lamu Island Retreat

4–8 Days Cultural & Leisure

Step back in time on this UNESCO World Heritage island. Narrow coral-stone streets, traditional dhow cruises, pristine beaches, and Swahili culture make Lamu Kenya's most enchanting and authentic escape.
